FACILITATOR GUIDE

The game is the lesson.

Students do not need to see a presentation first. Every mission introduces its own concept.

  1. Learn: Pick any tile and read its short field guide aloud. Invite a different student to read each time.
  2. Decide: Open the scenario. Let teams discuss A, B, or C and prepare a reason. Use the optional timer only after reading. Higher points are higher stakes; any tile can be played first.
  3. Discuss: Explore the outcome. Read why each choice works or falls short, then use “Try it together” to practice the skill. Award points for a choice and reasoning, not for memorizing vocabulary.
  4. Continue: Finish the mission to retire the tile. Undo restores its tile and previous scores. Play a selection of missions or the whole board.

For a first session, allow about 2–3 minutes per mission and adjust to discussion. Choose missions across categories; ten address relationships and sexual health.

All situations are fictional. Anyone may pass or observe, and no one should disclose personal health or relationship experiences. Keep the conversation respectful and inclusive of people who are not sexually active.

Facilitator references appear after each outcome. The supplied UNIV 101 material is the primary source, with linked health clarifications from CDC and current service information where checked. No diagnosis or personal medical plan is part of play.
